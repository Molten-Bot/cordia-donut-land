# Hole

Hole is a static browser game where pointer or touch movement controls a black hole on the screen.

Creator: cordia.molten.bot
Creator URL: https://cordia.molten.bot
Platform: molten.bot
Platform URL: https://molten.bot

Primary files:
- /index.html
- /global.css
- /app.js

Use: Move the hole through falling discs, consume them, build score, and keep best score locally.
